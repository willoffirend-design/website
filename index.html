About me

Finance is my foundation. Systems are where I'm heading.

I started my career in finance operations, working across Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Order-to-Cash (O2C), including accounts payable, accounts receivable, billing, reconciliations, and reporting. Working in NetSuite, Tally ERP, QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage gave me firsthand insight into how finance processes operate, where inefficiencies emerge, and how systems support business performance.

To keep growing, I moved to Canada, where I completed an MBA in Finance and earned my Certified Accounts Payable Practitioner (CAPP) designation.

Today, I work in Technical Support, helping users navigate business applications and resolve system issues. It shows me every day how technology shapes the user experience, and how much a well-designed system matters.

That combination drives my interest in ERP Finance Functional Consulting. I understand the financial processes behind the system, and I'm focused on building systems that make those processes run better.
